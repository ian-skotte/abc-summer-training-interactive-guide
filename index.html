What this is

Every summer, Benefits Administration walks ABCs through a full day (or two) of updates — special qualifying events, plan changes, retirement rules, billing, and more. This guide takes that training and turns it into something you can actually come back to in October, in the middle of a phone call, instead of digging through a slide deck or a transcript.

It covers both training days: Day 1 (State & Higher Education) and Day 2 (Local Education & Local Government). Where a rule is the same for everyone, it's just listed once. Where it isn't — deadlines, plan options, eligibility rules that genuinely differ by employer group — it's called out with a tag like this: Local Ed & Local Gov only

What you can do with it

Look something up fast

13 topic sections, from Special Qualifying Events to Billing to Retirement. Each one is a stack of short, specific answers — not a re-read of the whole transcript.

Filter to your group

Switch the sidebar filter to State & Higher Ed or Local Ed & Local Gov to hide everything that doesn't apply to your agencies.

Test what you know

Quiz mode pulls multiple-choice questions from the same content, with an explanation after every answer — good for onboarding a new ABC or a quick refresher before annual enrollment.

How to use it

  1. Set your audience filter

    At the top of the sidebar, choose "State & Higher Education," "Local Education & Local Government," or leave it on "All employer groups" to see everything, differences and all.

  2. Browse or search

    Click a topic in the sidebar to read through it, or type into the search box to jump straight to a specific rule, deadline, or dollar amount from any section.

  3. Switch to Quiz mode when you want to test yourself

    Use the Reference / Quiz toggle above the search box. In Quiz mode, pick "All topics" or a single topic, answer each question, and see an explanation before moving to the next one.

  4. Watch for the tags

    A pill like State & Higher Ed only on a card or question means that rule doesn't apply the same way to the other employer groups — worth a second look before repeating it to a member.

Good to know: this guide reflects what was presented in this year's training sessions. For anything with real stakes for a member — an appeal, an edge case, a number you're not fully sure of — confirm with the Active Service Center or your handout before you rely on it.
